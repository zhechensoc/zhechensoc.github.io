I am a Ph.D. student at Duke University in the [Department of Sociology](https://sociology.duke.edu/) and affiliated with the [Duke University Population Research Institute (DUPRI)](https://dupri.duke.edu/). I am also an affiliate doctoral student of the [International Max Planck Research School for Population, Health and Data Science (IMPRS-PHDS)](https://www.imprs-phds.mpg.de/) at the [Max Planck Institute for Demographic Research](https://www.demogr.mpg.de/en/). 

My research focuses on population health, aging, and health policy. 

A list of my publications can be found [here](research.md).
