I am a Ph.D. student at Duke University in the [Department of Sociology](https://sociology.duke.edu/) and an affiliated graduate student of the [Duke University Population Research Institute (DUPRI)](https://dupri.duke.edu/). 

My current research focuses on population health, aging, and health policy.

I will be presenting my research, *Does Contextual Social Trust Improve Productive Aging? A Multilevel Study of 22 European Countries*, at [PAA 2026](https://www.populationassociation.org/paa2026/home) in St. Louis, Missouri.
